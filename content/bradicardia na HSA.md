### Key points

- **Bradicardia isolada em HSA** pode resultar de **disfunção autonômica central (descarga [vagal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) reflexa)**, e **nem sempre indica hipertensão intracraniana**. 12
	- A **estimulação [vagal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** é mediada por estruturas como [insula](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) e [tronco encefálico](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), levando a **[bradicardia sinusal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) transitória** sem outros sinais de HIC. 23
- **Alterações eletrocardiográficas e disfunção cardíaca neurogênica** são comuns após HSA. 1
- O manejo deve incluir 
	- **exclusão de causas cardíacas ou metabólicas**, e,
	- se assintomática e estável, **não requer intervenção farmacológica imediata**. 23
		- **[Atropina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** é indicada apenas se houver **instabilidade hemodinâmica** (hipotensão, sintomas). 3

### Mecanismos possíveis

Nos pacientes com **hemorragia subaracnoide (HSA)**, a bradicardia pode surgir por múltiplos mecanismos:

1. **Reflexos autonômicos centrais:** aumento [súbito](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) da pressão intracraniana ativa o **reflexo de Cushing** (bradicardia, hipertensão e irregularidade respiratória) 4; contudo, a **bradicardia isolada** pode ocorrer por **estimulação [vagal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** sem aumento sustentado da PIC. 23
2. **Descarga catecolaminérgica e disfunção cardíaca neurogênica**, levando a alterações de repolarização e ritmo no [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) (T invertidas, [QT prolongado](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), [bradicardia sinusal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)).
3. **Efeitos farmacológicos** (sedativos, [betabloqueadores](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), [bloqueadores](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) de canal de cálcio). 2
4. **Distúrbios metabólicos** ([hipotermia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), [hiponatremia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), hipocalemia, hipotireoidismo) ou **[vasovagal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)**. 2

### Conduta recomendada

Deve-se seguir abordagem sistemática:

#### 1. **Avaliação inicial – abordagem ABCDE** 5
- Verificar via aérea, oxigenação, pressão arterial e [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) contínuo.
- Dosar eletrólitos, [TSH](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), troponina e realizar [ecocardiograma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) se suspeita de disfunção miocárdica neurogênica. 12

#### 2. **Exclusão de causas graves**
- **HIC aguda:** se ausente (sem hipertensão, alteração de consciência ou respiração irregular), a bradicardia isolada provavelmente é **reflexa ou autonômica transitória**.
- **Cardíaca:** excluir [infarto](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), bloqueios AV e disfunção ventricular relacionada a liberação de [catecolaminas](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#). 1
- **Metabólica:** corrigir [hipo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)/[hipernatremia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), distúrbios de potássio, ou temperatura corporal. 2

#### 3. **Manejo da bradicardia**

|Situação|Conduta|
|---|---|
|**Assintomática e hemodinamicamente estável**|Observar e monitorar [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) e PA. Corrigir causas reversíveis. 3|
|**Sintomática ou com hipotensão**|Administrar **[atropina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) IV** (1 mg, repetir a cada 3–5 minutos até 3 mg) e considerar **[dopamina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)/[epinefrina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** ou **pacing transcutâneo** se refratária. 23|
|**Farmacológica ou metabólica**|Suspender agentes causais e corrigir distúrbios. 2|

#### 4. **Monitorização**

- [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) contínuo, especialmente nas primeiras 72 h pós-HSA. 1
- Considerar **doppler transcraniano** para avaliar [vasoespasmo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), já que disfunção autonômica pode coexistir com [isquemia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) cerebral tardia. 1

### Interpretação clínica

Em um paciente com **HSA** e **bradicardia sem hipertensão ou alteração de consciência**, as causas mais prováveis são:
- **Reflexo [vagal](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) isolado ou descarga autonômica**,
- **Disfunção autonômica secundária à lesão cerebral**,
- **Efeito medicamentoso**.
Não se deve assumir aumento da pressão intracraniana sem outros sinais (hipertensão, padrão respiratório irregular). 24

### Passos práticos resumidos
1. **Monitorização contínua** ([ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), PA).
2. **Revisar medicamentos e eletrólitos.**
3. **Tratar se [bradicardia sintomática](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#):** [atropina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), considerar vasopressores [cronotrópicos](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#).
4. **Investigar disfunção miocárdica neurogênica** se alterações eletrocardiográficas.
5. **Procurar por causas estruturais cerebrais** apenas se houver deterioração neurológica subsequente.

### Takotsubo
- **[Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) (cardiomiopatia por estresse)** pode ser desencadeada por **hemorragia subaracnoide (HSA)**, configurando uma forma **secundária** da síndrome. 1
- Ocorre por **descarga maciça de [catecolaminas](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** → [vasoespasmo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) coronariano [difuso](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) e **“stunning” miocárdico transitório**, com disfunção regional do [VE](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) sem doença coronariana obstrutiva. 1
- **Apresentação clínica**: sinais de IC aguda, hipotensão/bradicardia, [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) com alterações isquêmicas (ST↑/T invertidas) e troponina levemente elevada. 1
- **[Ecocardiograma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** mostra padrão característico de **balonamento apical** (ou variantes midventricular/basal). 1
- **Tratamento** é de suporte, análogo à IC sistólica; **inotrópicos só com cautela** — evitados se houver **obstrução de via de saída do [VE](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) ([LVOT](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#))**. 1
- **Prognóstico**: recuperação completa em 1–2 semanas na maioria; mortalidade hospitalar até 5%; recorrência 2–4%/ano. 1

### Relação entre HSA e [Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)

A **HSA** é um dos principais **gatilhos neurológicos** do [Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#). O aumento abrupto da ativação simpática central e o pico plasmático de [catecolaminas](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) geram **toxicidade miocárdica direta** e **espasmo multivascular**. 1  
Essa forma — chamada **[Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) secundário** — tende a ocorrer em pacientes criticamente enfermos e pode manifestar-se por **bradicardia**, **hipotensão inexplicada** ou **[ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) anormal**, mesmo na ausência de dor torácica. 1

### Diagnóstico

**Critérios de Mayo modificados / escore InterTAK** são aplicáveis: 1

- Aumento leve de troponina/[CK-MB](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) (disproporcional à disfunção ventricular).
- [ECG](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) com **ST↑ [difuso](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)**, **T invertidas** e **[QTc](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) prolongado** (> 460 ms em mulheres).
- [Ecocardiograma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#): disfunção transitória do [VE](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) estendendo-se além de território coronariano único.
- Coronariografia: **sem obstrução significativa**.
- Exclusão de **miocardite** e **[feocromocitoma](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)**.

O **[InterTAK score](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** inclui “**distúrbio neurológico agudo**” (como HSA) como fator major (9 pontos), refletindo a forte associação cérebro-coração. 1

### Tratamento

#### Pacientes hemodinamicamente estáveis

- Tratar como IC sistólica: **IECA/[ARAII](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** e **[betabloqueador](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) em baixa dose ([metoprolol](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), [propranolol](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#), [esmolol](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#))**. 1
- Cautela: evitar hipotensão e excesso de betabloqueio em caso de bradicardia. 1
- **Profilaxia de [TEV](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)**: heparina não fracionada ou [enoxaparina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) se houver [hipocinesia](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) apical. 1

#### Hemodinamicamente instáveis

- **Sem [LVOT](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)**: pode-se usar inotrópico (levosimendana preferível; [dopamina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)/[dobutamina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) com risco de piora). 1
- **Com [LVOT](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** (até 25% dos casos): evitar inotrópicos e [vasodilatadores](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) — utilizar **[beta-bloqueador](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) de curta ação + volume + vasopressores puros** ([fenilefrina](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) ou vasopressina). 1
- Considerar **balão intra-aórtico, [ECMO](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) ou [LVAD](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** em choque refratário. 1

### Prognóstico e seguimento

- Recuperação da função ventricular em **1–2 semanas** é a regra. 1
- Recorrência anual de 2–4%; mortalidade hospitalar ~5%. 1
- Considerar **[beta-bloqueador](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) e IECA crônicos** para reduzir risco de recorrência. 1

### Integração com HSA

Nos pacientes com HSA:

- **Monitorização cardíaca contínua** nas primeiras 72 horas é essencial, pois o [Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) é causa de **instabilidade hemodinâmica não explicada por HIC ou [vasoespasmo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) cerebral**.
- A principal complicação é a **insuficiência cardíaca aguda neurogênica**, que agrava a perfusão cerebral — exigindo equilíbrio delicado entre suporte inotrópico e controle da pressão.
- A distinção entre [Takotsubo](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#) e **emiocardite “neurogênica” [difusa](https://next.amboss.com/us/ai-mode?cid=74d80f87-669d-47ac-9cd4-60768e99c4c2&sv=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22article%22%2C%22id%22%3A%22Bq0z0h%22%7D%5D&svi=0#)** baseia-se em imagem e evolução serial.
